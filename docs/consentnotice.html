Consent Notice



1. Acceptance of Consent
By accessing, browsing, registering, or using the website and services of Indian Art Association, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Consent Notice along with our Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and other applicable guidelines published on the website.

2. Consent to Information Collection
You voluntarily consent to the collection, storage, processing, and use of your personal information including your name, email address, phone number, city, institution details, artwork submissions, portfolio information, and other data provided during registrations, competitions, workshops, exhibitions, inquiries, or communication with the Association.

3. Consent to Communication
By submitting your contact details, you authorize Indian Art Association to contact you through email, phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, newsletters, or other communication methods regarding competitions, workshops, exhibitions, events, programs, announcements, updates, support services, and important organizational notifications.

4. Artwork & Creative Content Consent
By uploading or submitting artworks, photographs, videos, documents, portfolios, or any creative content through our platform, you confirm that you own the rights to such content or have proper authorization to share it. You grant Indian Art Association permission to display, publish, promote, or use submitted content for exhibitions, educational purposes, event promotions, social media activities, marketing materials, and organizational publications while maintaining appropriate credit to the creator whenever possible.

5. Consent for Event Participation
Participants registering for competitions, exhibitions, workshops, seminars, webinars, or cultural programs consent to the use of their names, participation details, photographs, videos, certificates, or event-related media for documentation, promotional, educational, and organizational purposes.

6. Consent to Website Technologies
Users consent to the use of cookies, analytics tools, and technical tracking technologies that help improve website performance, understand visitor behavior, enhance user experience, maintain security, and optimize website functionality.

7. Third-Party Services Consent
The website may contain links or integrations with third-party services, government portals, payment gateways, social media platforms, or partner organizations. By interacting with such services, users understand that separate terms and privacy policies of those third parties may apply.

8. Voluntary Participation
Participation in any competition, workshop, exhibition, or initiative conducted by Indian Art Association is completely voluntary. Users understand that participation does not guarantee awards, recognition, sponsorship, selection, or future opportunities unless specifically stated.

9. Data Protection Acknowledgment
While Indian Art Association takes reasonable steps to protect user information, users acknowledge that no online platform can guarantee complete security of data transmission or storage. By using the website, users accept this inherent risk.

10. Financial Consent & Awareness
Users understand that Indian Art Association does not conduct unauthorized financial transactions through its offices except official charges where applicable. Users agree to verify payment requests through official communication channels and report suspicious activities immediately.

11. Withdrawal of Consent
Users may request withdrawal of consent or request removal of their information from our records by contacting the Association through official contact channels. Certain information may still be retained where legally or operationally required.

12. Updates to Consent Notice
Indian Art Association reserves the right to update or modify this Consent Notice at any time without prior notice. Continued use of the website and services after updates indicates acceptance of the revised terms.
