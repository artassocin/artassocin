Privacy Policy



1. Introduction
Indian Art Association respects the privacy of every individual who visits our website or participates in our programs, competitions, workshops, and events. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your personal information in a responsible and transparent manner.

2. Information We Collect
We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, city, institution details, artworks, portfolio submissions, and any other information voluntarily provided by users while using our website or services.

3. How We Use Information
The information collected is used to manage registrations, organize competitions and events, provide updates, respond to inquiries, improve user experience, and support artists through our programs and initiatives.

4. Artwork & Content Submission
Artists submitting artworks, photographs, or creative content confirm that they own or have permission to share the content. Indian Art Association may use submitted content for promotional, educational, exhibition, or event-related purposes while ownership remains with the original creator.

5. Data Protection
We implement reasonable security measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, users understand that no online platform can guarantee complete data security.

6. Sharing of Information
Indian Art Association does not sell or trade personal information. Information may only be shared when required by law, for event coordination, verification purposes, or with trusted partners assisting in website operations.

7. Cookies & Analytics
Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to improve website functionality, understand visitor activity, and enhance user experience. Users may disable cookies through their browser settings.

8. Third-Party Links
Our website may contain links to government portals, partner organizations, or third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

9. Children’s Privacy
Participants under the age of 18 are encouraged to use the platform under parental, guardian, or institutional guidance.

10. Communication
Users may receive notifications regarding competitions, workshops, exhibitions, programs, announcements, and important updates through the contact information provided during registration or inquiry.

11. Financial Transactions Notice
Indian Art Association does not conduct unauthorized financial transactions through its offices except official charges where applicable. Users are advised to report any suspicious payment requests immediately.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with revised effective dates.

13. Contact Us
For privacy-related questions or support, contact us at:
Website: www.artassoc.in
Email: contact@artassoc.in
Support: support@artassoc.in
