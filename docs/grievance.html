Grievance Redressal Policy



1. Introduction
Indian Art Association is committed to maintaining transparency, professionalism, fairness, and accountability in all its activities, programs, competitions, workshops, exhibitions, and organizational operations. This Grievance Policy provides a structured process for users, participants, artists, organizations, and visitors to raise concerns or complaints related to our services or activities.

2. Purpose of the Grievance Policy
The purpose of this policy is to ensure that all grievances, concerns, complaints, or disputes are handled fairly, respectfully, confidentially, and within a reasonable timeframe.

3. Types of Grievances
Users may raise grievances related to:
- Competition registrations or participation
- Workshop or event management
- Certificates or recognition issues
- Misconduct or inappropriate behavior
- Website-related issues
- Unauthorized payment requests
- Intellectual property or content concerns
- Communication or support-related problems
- Any activity affecting the integrity of the Association

4. Submission of Grievances
All grievances should be submitted through official communication channels of Indian Art Association with complete details including the nature of the complaint, supporting information, screenshots, documents, or other relevant evidence wherever applicable.

5. Review Process
Upon receiving a grievance, the Association will review the complaint and may contact the concerned parties for clarification or additional information. Every effort will be made to handle grievances fairly, impartially, and efficiently.

6. Resolution Timeline
Indian Art Association aims to acknowledge grievances within a reasonable period and attempt to resolve them as quickly as possible depending on the complexity and nature of the issue.

7. Confidentiality
All grievance-related information will be handled with appropriate confidentiality. Personal information shared during the grievance process will only be used for investigation and resolution purposes.

8. False or Misleading Complaints
Users are expected to provide truthful and accurate information while submitting grievances. Indian Art Association reserves the right to reject complaints found to be false, misleading, abusive, or submitted with malicious intent.

9. Financial Fraud & Unauthorized Payments
Indian Art Association does not support unauthorized financial transactions through its offices except official charges where applicable. Users are encouraged to immediately report suspicious payment requests, fraud attempts, or misuse of the Association’s name.

10. Limitation of Responsibility
While the Association will make reasonable efforts to resolve complaints, submission of a grievance does not guarantee a specific outcome, compensation, or action unless deemed appropriate after review.

11. Changes to Grievance Policy
Indian Art Association reserves the right to update or modify this Grievance Policy at any time without prior notice. Continued use of the website or services indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact for Grievances
For grievance submissions or related concerns, contact us at:
Website: www.artassoc.in
Email: contact@artassoc.in
Support: support@artassoc.in
